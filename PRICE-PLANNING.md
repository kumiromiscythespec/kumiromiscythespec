# Monthly pricing worksheet

Use these notes before pricing an original worksheet pack, template or workbook. Fill the blanks by hand, keep a dated copy, and revise one assumption at a time. The aim is to compare a possible price with the costs you choose to include. A completed sheet is a planning scenario, not an earnings record. Copy this Markdown into your own local notes; it does not calculate or sync.

## Write five explicit inputs

| Input | Your assumption | What belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target amount left before tax | [amount] | A monthly amount after the costs included below; not confirmed profit. |
| Other monthly costs | [amount] | Costs beyond platform and processing fees. Include your work costs here if you want them covered. |

Blank means unknown. If no paid orders occur, a positive target or positive costs cannot be covered by sales in this model. Choosing a positive order count does not establish that buyers exist. Note where the order assumption came from: [fill in].

## Two fictional USD examples

Both cases assume $20 in other monthly costs, 10 paid orders and fictional processing of 2.9% plus $0.30 per order. Those processing figures are not a provider quote. The prices below were checked against the linked calculator's example results.

| Payhip plan | Case A: $100 target | Case B: $200 target |

| Plus | $15.99 per order | $26.50 per order |
| Pro | $22.87 per order | $33.17 per order |

These are cent prices meeting the target under the stated monthly model. Keep Free when it gives the lowest modeled price and meets your needs. Review an existing suitable store before opening another account or upgrading.
